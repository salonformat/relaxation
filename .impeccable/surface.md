## Direction contract
THESIS: A drawn place to arrive and stay, with optional short guidance. No scoring, timer UI or required outcome.
OWN-WORLD: Approved new illustrations derived from Isabella Kohout’s own drawings. Imperfect contours, broad color planes, fine ivory lines, darker muted colors. Josefin Sans and Della Respira. No photographs, no schematic toy landscapes, no browser speech.
STORY: Enter through a moving sea horizon, choose a place, confirm, arrive, then decide between simply staying and a short guide. Breath visibly moves water; body and senses guides leave generous silence.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cropped illustrated horizon and visible rolling waves. Large centered invitation, one entry control, language selector and optional rain/sound. German: “Bleib kurz hier.” English: “Stay a little while.” French: “Reste un peu.”
FORM: User-pinned immersive illustrated world. Everyday DE/EN/FR copy is maintained in dist/copy.js. Languages change without resetting the guide.
SIGNATURE: Canvas2D animated paint and broad water swells work without WebGL. Breath raises and lowers water and a light crest; window breathing changes daylight. No two-image crossfade. Pause stops animation; reduced motion lowers amplitude. Wind affects weather, fallen leaves and sound.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
