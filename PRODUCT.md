# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
First version: dependency-free HTML, CSS and Canvas/Web Audio JavaScript. Implementation choice inferred from the instruction to begin immediately; no standing framework preference established.

## Users
People whose day feels like too much, including the creator herself. They want somewhere to pause without learning, performing or achieving an outcome.

## Product Purpose
An immersive world that welcomes doing nothing. Working title: Heute ist alles ein bisschen viel.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Three entrances: briefly go elsewhere, set down a full head, simply stay. Choose sea, countryside or city; seasons and rain. Slowly moving worlds. Optional sound and reduced motion. No streaks, timers, completion, scoring or promises of therapeutic outcomes. A tangled wool ball can be set down, without needing to solve it. Local project and GitHub repository named relaxation requested.

## Brand Commitments
Salon Format is the creator's own design reference. Inspirations: Bobo Choses, Headspace, Calm, sea, rain, Casa Malaparte and the blue/red typography and cinematic aesthetic of Jean-Luc Godard, especially Le Mépris and Une femme est une femme. Original work; no copying brand characters or interfaces. Very little writing.

## Product Principles
- Nothing to finish, prove or learn.
- Worlds lead; controls recede.
- Choice without an exhausting setup.
- A full head may remain full.
- Leaving requires no evaluation.

## Confirmed refinements
- Photography and generic generated photographic aesthetics rejected.
- Perfectly geometric schematic scenes rejected as naive and unlike the creator.
- Existing portfolio images used as finished scenes also rejected. They are references for newly created illustrations, not substitute deliverables.
- New illustrations may be generated using the creator's own drawings as stylistic references. Follow imperfect contours, colored planes and delicate linework.
- German wording must be beautiful, natural and flowing, without constructed mindfulness language.

## Current experience
The user approved the illustrated assets, then requested a coherent arrival → choice → optional guidance flow. The current entrance uses an illustrated moving sea horizon and one invitation. All interface text, instructions, accessible labels and technique notes are available in German, English and French, written conversationally. The terrible synthetic narrator was explicitly removed. Water must visibly animate even without WebGL; overlapping scene fades were explicitly rejected. Optional breathing changes water, or daylight at the window, without measuring the visitor’s breath.


## Water and sound refinement · 2026-10-27
The entrance now has one sentence and the entry button. Water uses continuous two-pixel displacement samples from an isolated water texture, keeping land out of the animated layer. Seven expanding swells dissolve in perspective; a separate broad lift of the water surface follows the voluntary four-second in / six-second out rhythm. The original horizon and land remain still. Reduced motion attenuates displacement, and pause freezes the scene.
The procedural stereo soundscape has separate deep water, surf wash, foam, rain, foliage, wind and irregular drop layers. Slow, unequal currents vary gain and filtering; surf shares a time base with the visible water. These are synthesized sounds, not field recordings. Sound still requires user interaction and fades when the tab is hidden.
